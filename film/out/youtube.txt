TITLE
A decade of offshore production on one globe, from public records | Shell Asset Atlas (unofficial)

DESCRIPTION
Oil and gas operators report what they produce to public regulators every month. Shell Asset Atlas puts those records on a 3D globe: 210 installations and sites, 48 fields, 5,941 km of pipelines and a production history you can replay month by month.

Try it yourself: [add the link]

Everything in this video is the real application, filmed frame by frame, with the cursor pressing the real controls. The narration uses a synthetic voice (Kokoro). The music is synthesized.
